Likely questions

Tell me about yourself and why you're interested in this AI Developer Intern role at Dayforce.

Start with a brief overview of your academic background (Computer Programming & Analysis at Seneca Polytechnic) and your passion for AI and software development. Connect your interest directly to Dayforce's work in AI-powered HCM solutions and the specific responsibilities of this internship, such as building AI-driven workflows and automating testing. Highlight a relevant project like 'SweetScale' to showcase your AI/LLM skills. Mention your curiosity and initiative, aligning with Dayforce's culture.

Describe your experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and prompt engineering. How have you applied these concepts?

Discuss your experience with the Gemini API in your 'SweetScale' project. Explain how you designed prompts to achieve specific outputs for dessert recommendations based on user inputs (budget, guest count, theme). If you have theoretical knowledge beyond this project, mention generative AI concepts and how you understand they work. If you've experimented with other LLMs or techniques, briefly touch upon them.

You have experience with Python. How have you used it in projects, and how do you see it being used in an AI test automation platform?

Highlight the 'Olympic Data Processing' project where you used Python for data cleaning and standardization. Mention the caching implementation for performance improvement. For the AI test automation platform, explain how Python's libraries (e.g., for data manipulation, API interaction, machine learning) are crucial for backend development, data analysis of test results, and potentially integrating with AI models.

Can you explain your understanding of SQL and how it might be used in this role?

Mention your experience with SQL databases like PostgreSQL and Oracle SQL (from your resume). Explain that SQL is essential for interacting with databases to store, retrieve, and manage data. In this role, it could be used for storing test results, user data, configuration settings for the AI automation platform, or analyzing performance metrics related to automation effectiveness.

This role involves working with REST APIs. Can you describe your experience with them?

Refer to your projects like 'Book Search App' and 'Project Management App' where you likely interacted with or built REST APIs. Explain what a REST API is (e.g., a way for different software applications to communicate over the internet) and mention your familiarity with concepts like HTTP methods (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE), request/response formats (like JSON), and common status codes. Connect this to how the AI test automation platform might use APIs to integrate with other systems or services.

How would you approach identifying automation opportunities by collaborating with implementation consultants?

Emphasize your communication and problem-solving skills. State that you would start by actively listening to the consultants to understand their current manual testing workflows, pain points, and objectives. Ask clarifying questions about the steps involved, common errors, time spent, and areas that are repetitive or complex. Then, propose how AI or automation techniques could streamline these processes, perhaps by automating test case generation, execution, or result analysis, thereby saving time and improving accuracy.

Describe a challenging problem you faced in a project and how you solved it.

Choose a project from your resume, e.g., 'Olympic Data Processing' or 'Project Management App'. Detail the specific technical challenge (e.g., inconsistent data formats, complex CRUD logic, authentication issues). Explain your thought process, the steps you took to diagnose and resolve the problem, and the outcome. Highlight your analytical and problem-solving skills. If you encountered difficulties with an AI concept, mention that and how you overcame it through research or experimentation.

What are your thoughts on agentic AI frameworks and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)? How might they be used in test automation?

Showcase your understanding of these concepts, even if theoretical. Explain that agentic AI frameworks allow AI models to act more autonomously, plan tasks, and use tools. RAG enhances LLMs by providing them with external knowledge from a database (like test documentation or historical defect data) to generate more accurate and context-aware responses or actions. In test automation, RAG could help generate more relevant test cases based on specific software requirements, or agents could be used to automatically execute test scripts, analyze failures, and even suggest fixes.

How do you measure the effectiveness of automation, and what metrics would you track?

Refer to the job description's examples: 'implementation hours saved', 'test coverage improvements', and 'earlier defect detection'. Expand on these by explaining *how* you would measure them. For example, track the time spent on manual testing before automation vs. after, monitor the percentage of code covered by automated tests, and analyze the stage at which defects are found (e.g., in development vs. production). Also, consider metrics like reduction in manual testing effort, increase in test execution speed, and defect leakage rate.
