## Appendix: About Medium's Editorial Process

Medium's stated mission emphasizes "depth, nuance, and substance" but this is achieved through:

- Algorithm-based recommendation (not editorial selection)
- Member feedback (Medium's membership community)

**Critical Gap**: Medium explicitly does NOT employ editors to fact-check or vet individual articles for accuracy before publication. This is fundamentally different from traditional journalism or academic publishing.
