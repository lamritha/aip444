## Detailed Fact Verification

### 1. Coal Responsibility for 40% of Emissions
**Claim:** "coal - which is responsible for 40% of annual CO2 emissions"

**Verification:**
- ✓ CSIRO (Australian Commonwealth Scientific Research Organization): "In 2022, most of the world's fossil fuel carbon emissions came from coal (40 per cent)"
- ✓ Carbon Brief analysis: "In 2025, coal is responsible for more emissions than any other fossil fuel, representing approximately 42% of global fossil-fuel CO2 emissions"
- ✓ Center for Climate and Energy Solutions: "emissions from coal use is responsible for about 40 percent of global greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from fossil fuel use"

**Status:** ✓ ACCURATE

---

### 2. Methane Reduction Target: 30% by 2030
**Claim:** "A scheme to cut 30% of methane emissions by 2030 was agreed by more than 100 countries"

**Verification:**
- ✓ UN Climate Change: "103 countries, including 15 major emitters, signed up to the Global Methane Pledge, which aims to limit methane emissions by 30 per cent by 2030"
- ✓ PBS News: "More than 100 countries agreed to cut their methane emissions 30% by 2030 under the Global Methane Pledge"
- ✓ International Energy Agency: "111 country participants who together are responsible for 45% of global human-caused methane emissions"
- ✓ Global Methane Pledge official website: Confirms 30% target reduction from 2020 levels by 2030

**Status:** ✓ ACCURATE

---

### 3. Methane's Share of Warming
**Claim:** "Methane is currently responsible for a third of human-generated warming"

**Verification:**
- ✓ UN Climate Change: "Methane, one of the most potent greenhouse gases, is responsible for a third of current warming from human activities"
- ✓ PBS News cites Global Methane Assessment: Supporting data for methane's warming contribution

**Status:** ✓ ACCURATE

---

### 4. $100 Billion Climate Finance Pledge Missed
**Claim:** "after a previous pledge for richer countries to provide $100bn (£72bn) a year by 2020 was missed"

**Verification:**
- ✓ OECD analysis: "reached $79.6 billion in 2019" but "did not mobilise $100 billion jointly by last year's deadline"
- ✓ UN Climate Change: "Developed countries came to Glasgow falling short on their promise to deliver US$100 billion a year"
- ✓ Nature journal: "Twelve years ago... they promised to channel US$100 billion a year to less wealthy nations by 2020" but this "has not been achieved"
- ✓ IIED briefing: Confirms the $100 billion goal "was reaffirmed by the Paris Agreement in 2015 and extended to run to 2025" but "remains unmet"

**Status:** ✓ ACCURATE

---

### 5. $130 Trillion Financial Sector Commitment
**Claim:** "Financial organisations controlling $130tn agreed to back 'clean' technology, such as renewable energy"

**Verification:**
- ✓ UN Climate Change COP26 reporting: "The firms collectively control over $130 trillion in private capital"
- ✓ World Resources Institute: "Over 400 financial firms which control over $130 trillion in assets committed to aligning their portfolios to net-zero"
- ✓ Glasgow Financial Alliance for Net Zero (GFANZ): "Capital committed to net zero now at over $130 trillion"
- ✓ UN High-Level report: Confirms this represents a 25-fold increase from $5 trillion at start of COP26 presidency

**Status:** ✓ ACCURATE

---

### 6. Current Pledges Lead to 2.4°C Warming
**Claim:** "Current pledges, if met, will only limit global warming to about 2.4C"

**Verification:**
- ✓ Climate Action Tracker (backed by Potsdam Institute): "the world is heading for 2.4C of warming" based on current pledges
- ✓ UN Climate Change: References projections that pledges reduce warming to "2.4–2.6°C"
- ✓ BBC's own reporting corroborates this finding (BBC News article titled "COP26: World headed for 2.4C warming")

**Status:** ✓ ACCURATE

---

### 7. Coal Language Changed from Phase-Out to Phase-Down
**Claim:** "countries only agreed a weaker commitment to 'phase down' rather than 'phase out' coal after a late intervention by China and India"

**Verification:**
- ✓ Wikipedia (Glasgow Climate Pact): "A pledge to 'phase out' coal was changed to 'phase down' late in negotiation"
- ✓ UN Climate Change: "countries ultimately agreed to a provision calling for a phase-down of coal power... Many countries... expressed dissatisfaction that the language on coal was significantly weakened (from phase-out to phase-down)"
- ✓ UNFCCC official decision document: Language confirms "phasedown of unabated coal power"

**Status:** ✓ ACCURATE
