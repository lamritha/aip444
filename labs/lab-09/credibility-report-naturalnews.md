# Credibility Analysis Report: Natural News (naturalnews.com)

**Overall Credibility Rating: VERY LOW**

---

## Executive Summary

Natural News is a website operated by Mike Adams (known as "The Health Ranger") that has established a reputation as one of the internet's most prolific sources of health misinformation, conspiracy theories, and pseudoscience. Multiple fact-checking organizations, academic institutions, and social media platforms have identified this site as unreliable.

**Key Finding**: Natural News receives a **NewsGuard credibility rating of 0.0/100** (lowest possible score) for "severely violating basic standards of credibility and transparency."

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Author Analysis: Mike Adams ("The Health Ranger")

**Background:**
- Self-describes as "activist-turned-scientist" but documentation of formal scientific education is absent
- Founder of Natural News (2008) and owner of Consumer Wellness Center
- Also operates multiple other websites promoting conspiracy theories and alternative political content

**Credentials Issues:**
- No documented bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree in science, medicine, health, or related fields
- Claims scientific expertise through ownership of Consumer Wellness Center Labs (founded 2016), which uses ICP-MS instrumentation
- Operating laboratory equipment does not confer scientific credentials

**Professional Assessment:**
- Described by molecular geneticist Layla Katiraee as having "no legitimate science background"
- Criticized by oncologist David Gorski (Wayne State University): "Natural News and Mike Adams have been as harmful to public discourse on science, medicine, and also politics as Alex Jones"
- NBC News analysis identified Adams' operation as "by far the worst spreader of health misinformation online"

### 2. Publication Analysis

**Founding & History:**
- Founded in 2008 (formerly NewsTarget)
- Based in United States; Adams operates multiple domain network through Truth Publishing, LTD (registered in Taiwan/Arizona)
- Claims to publish 20 original articles daily

**Traffic & Reach:**
- Claimed 7 million unique visitors per month (2014)
- 6 million unique visitors reported as of 2016-2020
- Facebook had nearly 3 million followers before being banned

**Editorial Process:**
- **Self-published** - no independent editorial board, peer review, or fact-checking
- Articles bylined by Adams and freelance writers
- No transparency about editorial standards or corrections policies

**Reputation Assessment:**
- NewsGuard credibility rating: **0.0/100** (lowest tier; "severely violates basic standards of credibility and transparency")
- Described as "far-right website" by Wikipedia and multiple sources
- Institute for Strategic Dialogue: "prolific peddler of health disinformation and both medical and non-medical conspiracy theories"
- Vox (2020): "One of the internet's oldest and most prolific sources of health misinformation and conspiracy theories"

### 3. Platform Actions & Bans

| Platform | Action | Date |
|----------|--------|------|
| **Facebook** | Banned all links to Natural News and sister sites | June 2019, May 2020 |
| **Google** | Delisted from search results | February 2017 |
| **YouTube** | Restricted/demonetized | Ongoing |
| **Twitter** | Limited distribution/suspended some accounts | Ongoing |

**Reason Cited:** "Misleading or inaccurate information" and "fake news" per Facebook; promotion of false health claims per others.

### 4. Content Analysis: Major False Claims Documented

**Vaccine Misinformation:**
- **Claim:** MMR vaccine causes autism
- **Fact:** Multiple studies (including 2002 BMJ study cited by Natural News) found NO causal link; rejected by CDC, WHO, and all major health organizations
- **Evidence:** Natural News misrepresents sources to create false impression of scientific support

**COVID-19 Conspiracy:**
- Promoted false claims about vaccines containing microchips
- Spread "5G towers cause COVID-19" conspiracy
- Made unsubstantiated claims linking vaccines to death
- Identified in peer-reviewed study as top 5 most-shared fake news domains for COVID-19 content (Northeastern, Harvard, Northwestern, Rutgers)

**Climate Change Denial:**
- **Claim:** Wind turbines contribute more to climate change than fossil fuels
- **Fact:** Contradicted by extensive climate science; wind is renewable with low lifecycle emissions

**Zika Virus:**
- **Claim:** Genetically modified mosquitoes caused Zika epidemic
- **Fact:** No scientific evidence; Zika spread through natural mosquito populations

**Other Documented False Claims:**
- Chemtrails causing harm (unsupported)
- Fluoridated water causes specific health harms (contradicted by dental and public health research)
- Various dietary supplements can cure cancer (dangerous medical advice)
- Ancient civilizations on Mars (non-scientific)

### 5. Fact-Checking Verification

**Professional Fact-Checkers:**
- **Snopes:** Multiple articles flagged as false
- **AFP (Agence France-Presse):** Repeatedly debunked Natural News claims
- **Full Fact:** Identified false claims in fact-check reviews
- **FactCheck.org:** Associated Natural News with misleading information

**Academic Research:**
- 2020 study analyzing Natural News-affiliated domains found that fact-checking organizations "repeatedly flagged Natural News content as false"
- ISD (Institute for Strategic Dialogue) analyzed 10 most-shared Natural News articles: majority contradicted by fact-checkers

### 6. Financial Conflicts of Interest

**Revenue Streams:**
1. **Direct Product Sales:** Health Ranger Store selling supplements, organic products, prepper supplies
2. **Affiliate Marketing:** Partnerships with supplement companies and health product manufacturers
3. **Advertising:** Display ads on naturalnews.com
4. **Consultation/Lab Services:** Consumer Wellness Center Labs testing services

**Conflict of Interest:**
- **Content Bias:** Articles frequently promote products available in Health Ranger Store
- **Incentive Structure:** More fear-based health claims = higher engagement = higher product sales
- **Undisclosed Relationships:** Limited transparency about affiliate commissions
- **Self-Dealing:** Own lab operates with less regulatory oversight than traditional testing facilities

**Example:** Natural News article "Cancer industry not looking for a cure" (most-engaged cancer article 2019) conveniently promotes alternative cancer treatments available through Health Ranger ecosystem.

### 7. Network & Extremist Connections

**Website Network:**

- Content covers anti-government, militia, and "prepper" ideologies

**Associated Figures:**

## Fact-Check Examples

### Example 1: Vaccine-Autism Claim

**Natural News Article:** "When studies are conducted, there are links between vaccination and neurodevelopmental issues" (implies autism-vaccine link)

**Problem:** Embedded source (2002 BMJ study) actually found NO causal link between MMR and autism

**Assessment:** Misrepresentation of source to create false impression

**Mainstream Science:** CDC, WHO, NIH, medical schools worldwide: No causal link found in any rigorous study

---

### Example 2: Wind Turbines & Climate Change

**Natural News Claim:** Wind turbines contribute more to climate change than fossil fuels

**Evidence Against:**
- IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change): Wind has lifecycle emissions of ~10-15 g CO2/kWh
- Fossil fuels: ~400-1000 g CO2/kWh
- Nature-published studies: Wind power has lowest lifecycle emissions

**Academic Consensus:** Completely contradicted

---

### Example 3: Snopes Fact-Check

**Natural News Claims About Snopes:** Snopes is a "fake fact checker" accepting funding from "pedophiles" and Mark Zuckerberg

**Reality:** 
- Snopes is an established fact-checking organization with decades of history
- Snopes funding model disclosed; independent and transparent
- Natural News deflects from false claims by attacking the fact-checker

## Conclusion

Natural News operated by Mike Adams represents one of the most extensively documented sources of health misinformation on the internet. The site systematically:

1. Misrepresents scientific evidence
2. Promotes false health claims that contradict mainstream medicine
3. Profits from fear-based health narratives while selling alternative products
4. Operates under the guise of "science" without legitimate scientific credentials
5. Has been repeatedly banned by major platforms for spreading false information
6. Receives the lowest possible credibility rating from professional fact-checking organizations

The consistency and scale of documented misinformation, combined with multiple platform bans, academic criticism, and systematically failed fact-checks, makes this source wholly unreliable for factual information.
